<h2>🌱 Features</h2>
1. A dynamic website made with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Django, which allows authentication and handling of multiple users.
    
2. Adding, editing, removing, and completing tasks across to-do list boxes. Includes subtasks and adding details.

3. Refreshing tasks return every day to gently remind the user to move into a new day, with yesterday's completion being honestly recorded
    
4. Task analysis based on your activity, organized and displayed for the user's reflection. Includes heatmap, streak activity, completion chart.
    
5. Lumi, as an AI agent, can talk with you, motivate you, suggest your next steps, and offer advice, all customized and personalized by your real task data.
    
6. Finally, a guiding advice column — articles on motivation, long-term goals, efficient time management, and more to come!

<h2>🪻 Description</h2>
In a world pursuing immediate gratification and short-term achievements, DoFresh encourages persistent growth and celebrates daily achievements with its users, reframing the guilt of stagnant tasks and the vagueness of unrecorded consistency to empower and solidify their steps to reflection, growth, and self-motivated action.

While traditional task systems treat completion as an endpoint, this app features tasks that refresh every day, replacing the pressure of seemingly overdue items with daily-fresh beginnings. With every automated reset, it tracks the daily-reset tasks and records their dates of completion. Charts of actual behavior contextualize “I think I’ve been practicing well” and “I feel like I haven’t done anything this week” with concrete, visual feedback, informing users about completion patterns and streaks to make progress observable. An integrated chatbot offers reassurance and grounding advice that encourages users to take one step further or to take a re-energizing break, shaping realistic suggestions about next steps around the user’s situation, goals, and habits.

Through DoFresh, users can smile at small but real triumphs every day and commit persistently to long-term projects and goals without complacency or urgency.
