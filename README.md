<h2>DoFresh</h2>
<h5>A productivity tool for concrete persistency</h5>


In a world of pursuing immediate gratification and visible perfection, DoFresh encourages persistent growth and celebrates daily achievements with its users, removing the guilt of stagnant tasks and the vagueness of unrecorded consistency.

While traditional to-do apps press for one-time completions, this app features tasks that refresh every day, replacing the staleness of seemingly overdue items with daily-fresh beginnings. With every automated reset, it tracks the daily-reset tasks and records their dates of completion. Charts convert vague self-assessment into concrete, visual feedback, informing users about completion patterns and streaks without second-guessing or assumptions. Through DoFresh, users can stay grounded in their momentum, smile at small triumphs every day, and commit persistently to long-term projects and goals without complacency or urgency.
